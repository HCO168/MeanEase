## Morphology data

The current runtime schema contains dedicated morphology fields. Historical etymology text is no longer bundled into those fields, and the structure-only migration does not generate replacement morphology content.

## Tatoeba bilingual examples

English-Chinese sentence pairs are derived from the [Tatoeba Project](https://tatoeba.org/) through the [ManyThings bilingual export](https://www.manythings.org/anki/). The export snapshot is dated 2026-02-13 and contains native-speaker or proofread sentences selected by ManyThings. Each bundled example records its Tatoeba sentence page.

The sentence pairs are reused under [Creative Commons Attribution 2.0 France](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/) (CC BY 2.0 FR). Attribution is given to Tatoeba and ManyThings; individual contributor attribution is available on each linked Tatoeba sentence page.
